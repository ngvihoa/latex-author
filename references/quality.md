# Final quality checks

- Compilation: successful latexmk exit; current PDF exists; no undefined citations/references, missing characters, or unexplained overfull boxes. Read the log even when the build succeeds.
- Evidence: numbers agree across prose, tables, and figures; units and split names are explicit; illustrative data is clearly labeled.
- References: citations resolve and support their associated claims; bibliography entries are verified.
- Visual review: open every rendered final page; inspect margins, diacritics, table widths, figure resolution, captions, page numbering, headings, and blank pages. Text extraction alone cannot prove layout quality.
- Handoff: include source and necessary assets; record engine and build command. Exclude caches, private paths, credentials, and unrelated project data. Disclose unfinished checks instead of claiming readiness.
